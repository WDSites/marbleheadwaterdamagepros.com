Marblehead and the five nearby towns

Our home base is Marblehead, ZIP 01945, which includes Old Town, Marblehead Neck and Clifton. Water damage there often involves older homes, harbor-side streets and coastal storms, and the homepage covers the details.

Beyond Marblehead, each nearby community has its own page because its flooding, utilities and housing are different.

Swampscott and Salem

Swampscott, ZIP 01907, sits beside Marblehead along the coast and has its own seawall and flood-zone concerns on Humphrey Street. Salem, ZIP 01970, is across the Forest River and deals with river and harbor-edge flooding.

Lynn, Peabody and Beverly

Lynn has four residential ZIP codes (01901, 01902, 01904 and 01905) and many older multi-family buildings. Peabody, ZIP 01960, has brook-fed flooding near downtown, and Beverly, ZIP 01915, has a tidal river district and its own water supply board.

Who handles water, sewer and permits in each town

After a loss you may need to talk to the town, not only to a restoration crew. A burst service line, a sewer odor or a repair that needs a permit each points to a different office, and the offices differ by town. The table below lists them as the towns themselves describe them.

Each location page adds the local flooding, housing and utility details that matter for that town. If a pipe has burst, the first call for a service-line shutoff is the water provider, and the dispatch line can help you sort out which office to reach.

Which services are available

All six services are available across the service area: water mitigation, emergency water removal, flood damage restoration, water damage cleanup, water damage repair and mold remediation. The services overview explains how to tell them apart.

Outside these towns
