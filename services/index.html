Water damage rarely happens in one step. Water arrives, gets removed, the structure dries, and then walls and floors are rebuilt. Each stage has its own service, and the right one depends on how far along you are.

Our dispatch line is open 24/7 and sends licensed and insured professionals. Below is what each service does and when to choose it.

Water damage repair

Once everything is dry, the rebuilding starts. Water damage repair replaces drywall, insulation, flooring, trim and paint that could not be saved. Choose it when the water is gone and you need the house put back together.

Water mitigation

Mitigation is about limiting the damage in the first days. Water mitigation means finding wet materials with moisture meters, drying them with air movers and dehumidifiers, and stopping the spread to dry rooms. It fits best when a leak is already fixed but walls or floors still feel damp.

Water damage cleanup

Cleanup deals with what water leaves behind. Water damage cleanup covers removing wet debris, cleaning and treating surfaces, and sorting belongings that can be saved. It suits you when the worst is over but the mess and the smell remain.

What to do before you choose a service

  • Stop the water at its source if it is safe to do so, because every service below assumes the source is controlled.
  • Keep out of rooms with sagging ceilings, sewage or standing water near electrical outlets.
  • Take photos of the damage before anything is moved, since they help with any later insurance conversation.
  • Call the dispatch line and describe what you see, and it will point you to the right service.

Emergency water removal

When water is standing or still coming in, time matters. Emergency water removal pumps and extracts water at any hour. Pick it when you have an active flood at night or on a weekend.

Flood damage restoration

Flood water from storms, tides or sewer backup is contaminated, so the work is different. Flood damage restoration addresses Category 3 water, with more material removal and disinfection. Choose it after a coastal storm or when water came up through a drain.

Mold remediation

Mold follows moisture. Mold remediation contains the affected area, removes damaged materials and dries what remains, following S520 practice. Choose it when you have found visible mold or a musty area after a past leak.
